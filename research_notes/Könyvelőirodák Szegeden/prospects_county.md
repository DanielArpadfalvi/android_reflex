# Könyvelőirodák in Csongrád-Csanád County, outside Szeged city: prospect list (as of Oct 2026)

**Scope:** Accounting firms (TEÁOR 6920) seated in Csongrád-Csanád county outside Szeged city, for a Szeged-based AI-automation Kft looking for first interview partners or pilot customers. No firm was contacted.

**Method and reliability note (read first):**
- The sandbox's egress proxy blocked direct page fetches. That covered the firms' own websites and directories such as kwotax.hu, roiroda.hu, csmkik.hu and konyveloirodafelgyo.hu. **Every fact below therefore comes from web-search result snippets.** The search tool also returns them partly summarised, so all facts are tagged **[S]** (snippet-derived, not verified against the live page). Before outreach, re-check each fact on the cited URL.
- Financials are from nemzeticegtar.hu, opten.hu, ceginfo.hu, tudjukki.hu and companywall.hu as they appeared in snippets. Size bands such as "3-4 fő" are registry bands, not exact headcounts.
- **Google review counts and ratings could not be collected for any firm**, because Maps and Google pages were not fetchable. This is a gap for every firm.
- Coverage is deliberately limited to firms with at least a minimal public footprint. The county has many more sole-trader bookkeepers: joszaki.hu claims about 100 in Makó and about 152 in Szentes ([joszaki Makó](https://joszaki.hu/szakemberek/konyveles/mako), [joszaki Szentes](https://joszaki.hu/szakemberek/konyveles/szentes) [S]).
- No Bács-Kiskun (Kiskunfélegyháza, Kiskunmajsa) firms were added, because the county list reached more than 20 entries.

## Cross-cutting observations (inferences)
- **The first three to approach are Ró (Hódmezővásárhely), Professional Analysis (Felgyő) and Kalkulus (Makó).** Respectively, they offer digital leadership with MinKE influence, an agricultural niche with 7 FTE, and a 15-person team. **Ancsi Kft (Algyő)** is the clearest "capacity pain" signal: it is hiring for invoice data entry.
- Most county firms are micro-sized: 3-4 employees and 11-25 M HUF revenue. Only Ró (123 M), City Tax (89 M), Professional Analysis (79 M), Kalkulus (46 M) and Tanácsadó, Könyvelő és Szolgáltató (41 M) exceed 40 M HUF.
- Agricultural specialisation is explicit at Professional Analysis, AB-LF, Kutás Vanda and BA Kontír; grant work at Hunyadi, Sikeres Jövő and AB-LF.
- Job-ad evidence is thin: one firm-specific ad (Ancsi) and one unattributed "könyvelőiroda keresi munkatársait" ad on profession.hu for Hódmezővásárhely ([profession](https://www.profession.hu/allasok/penzugy-konyveles/hodmezovasarhely/1,17,29,0,0,6800)). Other accountant ads in Hódmezővásárhely were from non-firms: the Hódmezővásárhely–Makó health centre, the HVSZ Zrt. municipal company and Duna Aszfalt ([hvszzrt](https://www.hvszzrt.hu/hir/hirdetes-63), [indeed](https://hu.indeed.com/q-k%C3%B6nyvel%C5%91-k%C3%B6nyvel%C5%91-l-h%C3%B3dmez%C5%91v%C3%A1s%C3%A1rhely-%C3%A1ll%C3%A1sok.html)) [S].
