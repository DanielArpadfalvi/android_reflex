The search budget ran out before every firm could be enriched.
>
> Only firms that have a seat (székhely) or an office in **Szeged city** are listed, including Kiskundorozsma (6791). Contact data is limited to the general business channels the firms publish themselves; for anything else, use the website's contact page. Nobody was contacted.

## Takeaway
I identified **31 accounting firms with a seat or office in Szeged** and noted 6 more as excluded or uncertain. The strongest pilot candidates are owner-led firms with roughly 5–19 staff that show digital signals and are hiring: **EU-TAX Consulting, GoldenEggs, Kadmin Plus, Prémium Audit, Top Controll, Tabula Rasa, Dr. Szetei Consulting, Adókontó 2000 and Stabil Kontír**. **JOY'S UNITED** is a special case. It is a Chinese-led firm with the highest revenue in the set and is hiring, so it fits the "foreign/Chinese clients" angle.
