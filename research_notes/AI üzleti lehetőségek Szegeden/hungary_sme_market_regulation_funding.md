# Hungarian SME (KKV) market for AI & automation services, 2025-2026: demand, regulation, funding

Research date: 2026-10-03. Method note: many primary pages (portfolio.hu, ivsz.hu, digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu, penzcentrum.hu, voszport.com) were blocked by the research environment's network proxy, so some figures come from search-engine extracts of those pages rather than full-text reads. These are marked "(snippet)" and should be checked before anyone quotes them.

## 1. SME structure, digital maturity, AI adoption, labour costs

### Takeaway
Hungary has around 900k SMEs, and about 96% of them are micro firms. Hungary sits near the bottom of the EU on digital and AI adoption: about 10% of enterprises used AI in 2025 against an EU average of 20%, and about 60% of SMEs reach basic digital intensity against an EU average of 73%. Wages keep rising fast (+9% a year, minimum wage +11% in 2026), and about 1 in 5 firms has posts it cannot fill. Together these give a strong economic case for automation, but the starting point is low and firms lack skills.

### Cited Findings
- Between 2014 and 2024 the number of SMEs (including micro firms) rose from 632k to 894k. Firms with 10-249 employees rose from 35.4k to 42k. The headline is "never this many SMEs, yet their economic weight keeps shrinking" (2026-08) — [Portfolio/KRTK](https://www.portfolio.hu/krtk/20260829/soha-nem-volt-ennyi-kkv-magyarorszagon-megis-egyre-kisebb-a-gazdasagi-sulyuk-858666) (snippet)
- In 2024 there were 1,810,272 *registered* enterprises (this count includes sole proprietors and inactive firms). Compared with 2021, micro firms fell 2.6%, small firms rose 7.7%, medium firms rose 6.7% and large firms rose 7.6%. Micro firms (0-9 employees) are about 96% of all firms — [KSH STADAT gsz0004](https://www.ksh.hu/stadat_files/gsz/hu/gsz0004.html); [novekedes.hu / Csath](https://novekedes.hu/elemzesek/hiaba-a-sok-ceg-megszunes-meglepetesre-par-agazatban-nott-a-vallalatok-szama) (snippet). Note: the registered count (1.8M) and the operating SME count (about 0.9M) measure different things.
- **Eurostat 2025:** 20.0% of EU enterprises with 10+ employees used AI (Denmark 42.0%, Finland 37.8%; Romania 5.2% and Poland 8.4% lowest). **Hungary: 10.4%**, about half the EU average — [Eurostat news 2025-12-11](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/products-eurostat-news/w/ddn-20251211-2); [Eurostat Statistics Explained](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Use_of_artificial_intelligence_in_enterprises). (The Hungary figure comes from a search extract.)
- **Digital Decade 2025 country report (June 2025, mostly 2024 data):** 59.81% of Hungarian SMEs have at least basic digital intensity (EU 72.9%), growing 6.1% a year from 2023 to 2025. 5.54% have very high digital intensity. AI use among firms is 7.4% (EU 13.5%; this is the Eurostat 2024 wave). Use of AI lags "in particular for SMEs, due to a lack of digital skills." Cloud use is 37.1%, close to the EU average. Hungary has no digital unicorns — [EC Digital Decade 2025 Hungary](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/factpages/hungary-2025-digital-decade-country-report); [SWD(2025)294 PDF](https://ec.europa.eu/transparency/documents-register/api/files/SWD(2025)294_12/090166e51e13f2b5); [Hungary Today](https://hungarytoday.hu/hungarys-digital-leap-hits-a-speed-bump-with-smes-and-5g/) (snippet)
- Another source says only 23% of Hungarian SMEs use cloud, against an EU average of 42% — [socialpro.hu / szamlazoprogramom](https://www.socialpro.hu/kkv-digitalizacio-2025-fordulopont/). **This conflicts** with the EC's 37.1%. The secondary source probably uses a different year or base, so the Eurostat/EC figure is the safer one.
- Population-level AI use (Microsoft "Global AI Adoption in 2025"): the share of working-age Hungarians using AI rose from 27.9% to 29.8% between H1 and H2 2025, ranking Hungary 19th in the world — [Microsoft Hungary 2026-01-21](https://news.microsoft.com/hu-hu/2026/01/21/magyarorszag-stabilan-a-top-20-orszag-kozott-az-ai-hasznalat-tekinteteben/); [Index](https://index.hu/techtud/2026/01/23/mesterseges-intelligencia-ai-magyarorszag-microsoft-jelentes-2025/)
- **Wages:** the gross average wage for full-time employees was HUF 756,400 in Nov 2025, up 8.9% year on year — [KSH gyorstájékoztató](https://www.ksh.hu/gyorstajekoztatok/ker/ker2511.html). The minimum wage rose from HUF 290,800 (2025) to **HUF 322,800 (2026, +11%)**. The guaranteed wage minimum for skilled workers rose from HUF 348,800 to **HUF 373,200 (+7%)** — [berfigyelo.hu](https://berfigyelo.hu/minimalber); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/hu/hu/hirek/tudastar/minimalber-2026-brutto-es-netto-oesszegek)
- **Labour shortage:** in the MKIK GVI autumn survey (about 6,700 firms), 20% of companies reported vacancies they had been unable to fill for a long time — [GVI munkaerőpiaci prognózis](https://gvi.hu/kutatas/744/rovidtavu-munkaeropiaci-prognozis-a-2025-os-evre-tavaszi-adatfelvetel); a 2026 spring wave also exists: [GVI 768](https://gvi.hu/kutatas/768/rovidtavu-munkaeropiaci-prognozis-a-2026-os-evre-tavaszi-adatfelvetel) (snippet; exact wave and year to be checked)

### Inferences
- Employer cost of one minimum-wage worker is about HUF 380k/month (gross 322,800 plus 13% social contribution tax). For a skilled worker it is about HUF 420k+, and for an average white-collar worker about HUF 850k. An automation service priced at HUF 50-150k/month that saves 0.25-0.5 FTE of administrative work pays back easily.
- Hungary trails the EU by about 10 percentage points on AI adoption, so the market is early-stage. The main barrier is skills and know-how, not technology. That favours "done-for-you" implementation and training over self-serve SaaS.
- Because micro firms dominate (96%), the realistic paying segment is the roughly 40-42k small and medium firms with 10-249 employees, plus professional-service micro firms such as accountants, law firms and clinics.

### Gaps
- I could not fetch the KSH breakdown of SMEs by sector or region (Csongrád-Csanád / Szeged).
- Eurostat 2025 AI adoption by size class for Hungary (small vs medium) was not retrieved.

## 2. Administrative and operational pain points; attitudes to AI

### Takeaway
The main pains are tax and reporting bureaucracy (NAV online invoicing, the new eÁFA/M-sheet detail, and receipt-data reporting from Sept 2026), labour shortage, and rising wage costs. SMEs are curious about AI but say they lack the expertise: in a VOSZ-linked survey, 88% named lack of expertise as the main problem. Employees already use ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude informally, mostly without company rules.

### Cited Findings
- A survey of about 500 Hungarian SMEs on AI, published around 2025: **88%** named lack of expertise as the main problem, and **92%** know AI tools at most to a medium level. The survey covered fears, knowledge, expectations and training needs — [VOSZ Port](https://voszport.com/kkv-mesterseges-intelligencia/) (snippet; full text was blocked)
- GKI survey, July 2025, 390 firms: **32%** use some AI-based software (services 53%, trade 42%). Employees' self-initiated AI use rose from 13% to 35% over the year. The most used tools were ChatGPT 69%, Gemini 56%, Claude 37% and Copilot 29% — [Prim hírek 2025-11-25](https://hirek.prim.hu/cikk/2025/11/25/mesterseges_intelligencia_a_magyar_cegeknel_hol_tartunk_2025-ben); [Piac&Profit](https://piacesprofit.hu/cikkek/kkv_cegblog/nincs-mese-tenyleg-rakaptak-a-magyar-cegek-a-mesterseges-intelligenciara-.html) (snippet). Note: this sample is self-selected and skews larger than Eurostat's, which explains why it is much higher than Eurostat's 10%.
- "The majority of firms use AI without any internal rules" — [Store Insider](https://storeinsider.hu/cikk/a-cegek-tobbsege-szabalyozas-nelkul-hasznalja-az-ai-t) (headline only)
- MKIK GVI: firms are calling for general burden relief on tax, bureaucracy and energy prices. 12.2% plan to cut headcount after the minimum-wage increases — [GVI minimálbér 2025 study](https://gvi.hu/files/researches/771/MKIK%20GVI%20Minim%C3%A1lb%C3%A9r%202025%20-%20tanulm%C3%A1ny.pdf) (snippet)
- **NAV pressure, 2026:**
  - The eÁFA M2M 2.0 XSD became mandatory in production on 3 Aug 2026.
  - eÁFA is optional now but becomes de facto mandatory once ÁNYK is phased out from **1 Jan 2027**.
  - The new 2665 VAT return form and a new M-sheet require detail on deductible VAT.
  - Sources: [PwC Hungary 2026](https://www.pwc.com/hu/hu/sajtoszoba/2026/adodigitalizacios_valtozasok.html); [ado.hu](https://ado.hu/ado/kozeleg-az-anyk-kivezetese-megjelent-a-2665-os-afabevallas-tervezete-nav-figyelo-49-het/); [Accace](https://www.accace.hu/afabevallas-m-lap-valtozas/)
- **Receipt-data reporting (nyugta-adatszolgáltatás):**
  - Mandatory for every business that must issue receipts from **1 Sept 2026**. The deadline was postponed from 1 July 2025.
  - It can be met through online invoicing, an online cash register or an e-cash register.
  - No penalties until 31 Dec 2026.
  - Online cash registers can be used until 30 June 2028.
  - Sources: [hirlevel.egov.hu](https://hirlevel.egov.hu/2025/03/01/kitolja-az-e-nyugta-bevezetesenek-hataridejet-a-kormany-csak-2026-szeptemberetol-lesz-kotelezo-a-hasznalata/); [5percado](https://5percado.hu/nyugta-adatszolgaltatas-szeptember-1-jetol-nem-az-e-penztargep-a-kotelezo-hanem-az-adat-dr-sztanko-daniel/); [Accace](https://www.accace.hu/nyugtaadat-szolgaltatas/)
- The NAV 2026 audit guideline focuses on VAT, risk analysis and data-driven audits — [tudatos-konyveles.hu](https://tudatos-konyveles.hu/nav-ellenorzesi-iranyelv-2026/)

### Inferences
- Concrete use cases that match the pains:
  - Invoice and receipt intake with OCR into accounting.
  - Reconciling NAV online-invoice data with the books before submitting eÁFA.
  - HR and roster automation that responds to labour shortage and wage pressure.
  - Hungarian-language customer service and email triage.
  - Internal AI usage policies, given widespread "shadow AI".
- Accounting offices (könyvelőirodák) are a leverage channel. They carry the eÁFA, ÁNYK and receipt-data changes for hundreds of micro clients each.

### Gaps
- I did not find primary 2025-26 PwC, KPMG or Deloitte Hungary CEO survey figures, or a KAVOSZ survey on pain points.
- There is no quantitative ranking of administrative burdens such as hours per month spent on NAV compliance.
- The KATA changes (since 2022, KATA is limited to sole proprietors selling to private individuals) were not re-verified for 2025-26.

## 3. Regulatory forcing functions, 2025-2028

### Takeaway
Hard, dated obligations create demand. In order of date and scale:
- **NIS2:** first audits due 30 June 2026 for about 3-4k+ entities, with supply-chain effects.
- **Accessibility Act:** in force from 28 June 2025 for e-commerce and websites.
- **AI Act:** AI literacy since Feb 2025, and a Hungarian implementing law with authorities since Dec 2025.
- **Receipt-data reporting and eÁFA:** 2026-27.
- **ESG/CSRD:** largely postponed. SMEs are exempt from ESG data requests until mid-2027.

### Cited Findings
- **NIS2 / Kiberbiztonsági törvény (2024. évi LXIX.):**
  - Organisations operating before 1 Jan 2025 had to sign a contract with an SZTFH-registered auditor by **31 Aug 2025**. SZTFH checks contracts from 15 Sep 2025.
  - The first cybersecurity audit was due by **30 June 2026**.
  - Sources: [SZTFH tájékoztatás](https://sztfh.hu/tajekoztatas-a-kiberbiztonsagi-auditra-vonatkozo-hatarido-modositasarol-valamint-a-szerzodeskotesre-vonatkozo-hataridorol/); [Privátbankár](https://privatbankar.hu/cikkek/vallalat/kiberbiztonsagi-audit-valtozott-a-hatarido-.html); [Jogtár](https://net.jogtar.hu/jogszabaly?docid=a2400069.tv)
- NIS2 scale:
  - Registration with SZTFH closed on 30 June 2024, with 3,000-3,800+ registrations.
  - Market estimates put the number of affected entities at up to 6,000.
  - Telex estimated the minimum audit market at HUF 5.5bn plus VAT, with an annual supervisory fee on top.
  - Sources: [Telex 2025-02-02](https://telex.hu/techtud/2025/02/02/nis2-kiberbiztonsagi-audit-sztfh-rendelet-dijak-reszletszabalyok-hunguard); [Andersen](https://hu.andersen.com/hu/hirek/nis2-iranyelv-kiberbiztonsagi-audit/); [RSM](https://www.rsm.hu/blog/nis2-hatarido-jelentos-szankciok)
- **EU AI Act, Hungarian implementation:**
  - Parliament adopted the implementing law on **21 Oct 2025**, in force **1 Dec 2025**.
  - The notifying authority is the Nemzeti Akkreditáló Hatóság. Market surveillance sits with the minister responsible for enterprise development. A Hungarian AI Council (Magyar MI Tanács) was set up.
  - Implementing decree: 344/2025. (X. 31.) Korm. rendelet.
  - The rules apply to any business that develops, integrates, distributes *or uses* AI. SMEs get lower fine ceilings.
  - Sources: [MKIK](https://mkik.hu/hirek/alkalmazando-az-ai-act-ujabb-merfoldkove); [NJT 344/2025](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2025-344-20-22); [AmCham](https://www.amcham.hu/media/amcham-news/mesterseges-intelligencia-szabalyozasa-magyarorszagon-amit-mindenkinek-tudnia)
  - Art. 4 AI literacy has applied since 2 Feb 2025 and GPAI obligations since 2 Aug 2025 (general AI Act timeline). The high-risk obligations were originally due Aug 2026, but the EU "Digital Omnibus" proposed delays. **I did not verify the final 2026 status** of that delay.
- **Accessibility (EAA transposition):**
  - The Hungarian act on accessibility of products and services has applied from **28 June 2025**.
  - It covers e-commerce, websites and apps of digital service providers, banking, telecoms, e-books and similar.
  - Services lawfully provided before that date have until **28 June 2030**.
  - Micro-enterprises providing services are exempt (Aktv.; detailed conditions not retrieved).
  - Sources: [HBKIK](https://hbkik.hu/digitalis-akadalymentesseg-uj-vilag-jon-junius-28-tol/); [Net-Jog](https://net-jog.hu/2025/06/11/termekek-es-szolgaltatasok-akadalymentessege-mit-jelent-ez-a-magyar-webshopok-szamara/); [PROAB](https://proab.hu/proab-news/webaruhazak-es-digitalis-szolgaltatasok-akadalymentesitese-uj-szabalyok-junius-28-tol/)
- **ESG:**
  - The ESG law (2023. évi CVIII.) set up the Hungarian ESG due-diligence framework. It was amended in 2025 after the EU Omnibus I.
  - Large companies' first certified ESG report moved to **2028**. SMEs are exempt from ESG data provision until **mid-2027**.
  - An SME's ESG data could be replaced by a state ESG rating; otherwise the buyer company sends a supplier questionnaire.
  - Omnibus I delayed CSRD by 2 years and CSDDD by 1 year.
  - Sources: [hvg.hu](https://hvg.hu/kkv/20250321_esg-halogatas-kkv-vallalkozasok-fenntarthatosagi-jelentese-hatarido-hosszabbitas-ngm-mkik-sztfh); [PwC](https://www.pwc.com/hu/hu/sajtoszoba/2025/modosulhat_az_esg_torveny.html); [KPMG blog](https://blog.kpmg.hu/2025/06/az-omnibusz-kovetkezo-megalloja-magyarorszag/); [RSM](https://www.rsm.hu/blog/fenntarthatosag-es-esg/omnibus-valtozik-a-csrd-es-a-csddd-2026-ban)
- **Tax digitalisation:** receipt-data reporting from 1 Sep 2026, and ÁNYK phase-out with eÁFA from 1 Jan 2027 (sources in section 2).

### Inferences
- NIS2 is the strongest forcing function, but it mostly hits medium and large firms in covered sectors. The opportunity for a small AI shop is subcontracting gap remediation, documentation, log monitoring and security-awareness training, and serving the NIS2 entities' *suppliers*. A small shop cannot be a registered auditor without SZTFH registration.
- AI Act literacy (Art. 4) plus the new Hungarian authority give a natural "AI policy + staff training" product. This matters because GKI found 35% of employees using AI on their own, mostly without rules.
- The EAA creates web and app accessibility remediation work for webshops (small firms, not micro).
- ESG is postponed and is a weak driver for SMEs before 2027-28.

### Gaps
- I did not research the Hungarian whistleblowing law (2023. évi XXV., applies to firms with 50+ employees), GDPR/NAIH enforcement trends, or Hungarian positions on mandatory domestic B2B e-invoicing and the ViDA timeline (EU ViDA: digital reporting requirements for intra-EU trade from July 2030).
- The final status of the EU Digital Omnibus delay to AI Act high-risk obligations is unverified.

## 4. Public funding for SME digitalisation and AI; can a provider be the implementer?

### Takeaway
Funding is plentiful. DIMOP Plusz explicitly lists AI-based solutions, and the 1.2.6/B-26 call offered HUF 3-12M at up to 90% intensity outside Budapest, which includes Szeged (Dél-Alföld). The Demján Sándor Program put HUF 1,410bn into SMEs in 2025, and Széchenyi Card investment loans run at 3.5%. Calls are grant- or loan-based, so the SME is the beneficiary and buys from suppliers. An AI provider positions itself as the **supplier/implementer**, often together with a grant-writing consultancy.

### Cited Findings
- **DIMOP Plusz-1.2.6/B-26** ("KKV-k növekedési lehetőségeinek támogatása a digitális infrastruktúra és transzformáció elősegítésére, II. kör"):
  - Total budget HUF 30bn.
  - HUF 3-12M per firm at up to 90% grant intensity.
  - Covers software, services and IT/network devices.
  - The project must be outside Budapest (less developed regions incl. **Dél-Alföld**).
  - A parallel **1.2.6/C-26** call covers Budapest with a HUF 2bn budget.
  - One aggregator lists B-26 as "closed (lezárult)", so this call's window has likely passed.
  - Sources: [palyazat.gov.hu közlemény](https://www.palyazat.gov.hu/kozlemenyek/megjelent-a-kkv-k-novekedesi-lehetosegeinek-tamogatasa-a-digitalis-infrastruktura-es-transzformacio-elosegitesere-ii-kor-cimu-dimop-plusz-1-2-6-b-26-kodszamu-felhivas); [Veszprémi Kamara](https://veszpremikamara.hu/hirek/megjelent-a-90-os-tamogatasi-intenzitasu-digitalizacios-palyazat-a-dimop-plusz-126b-26); [Pénzcentrum 2026-03-28](https://www.penzcentrum.hu/vallalkozas/20260328/itt-az-uj-dimop-plusz-kkv-palyazat-akar-12-millio-forintot-is-kaphatnak-a-cegek-digitalis-infrastruktura-fejlesztesre-1196062); [socialpro C-26](https://www.socialpro.hu/blog/dimop-c-26-budapesti-kkv-palyazat-2026/); [magyarfalupalyazat (closed)](https://magyarfalupalyazat.hu/palyazatok/digitalizacios-tamogatas-mikro-kisvallalkozasok-dimop-2026)
- **DIMOP Plusz-1.2.3/A-24** is a combined loan and grant product of HUF 3-20M. Eligible items explicitly include e-invoicing, cloud, remote-work tools, ERP, CRM *or AI-based technologies and services* — [Index 2025-07-02](https://index.hu/gazdasag/2025/07/02/palyazat-unios-forrasok-ginop-plusz-beruhazas-fejlesztes-tamogatas-fejlesztespolitika/); [BKIK](https://bkik.hu/hirek/hirek/dimop-plusz-palyazatok) (snippet)
- **DIMOP Plusz-5.1.2/B** is for SMEs that develop new digital technology themselves, including AI, data, cybersecurity and cloud/edge (STEP-critical technologies). It is relevant if the AI provider itself applies as a developer — [BKIK](https://bkik.hu/hirek/hirek/erkeznek-a-dimop-plusz-palyazatok) (snippet)
- A national SME digital-maturity assessment run by DKF Kft. under DIMOP Plusz can be a precondition for later development grants — [Adózóna](https://adozona.hu/altalanos/Felmerik_a_kkvk_digitalizacios_fejlettseget_UHZ0LX); [kkvdigital.dkf.hu](https://kkvdigital.dkf.hu/?module=projektek)
- **Demján Sándor Program** (Government + MKIK):
  - HUF **1,410bn** to SMEs in 2025, in nine elements.
  - Elements include a capital programme, the "1+1" investment programme, an SME digitalisation package, export credit, EU development programmes, and cuts to administrative burden.
  - The website programme "Minden vállalkozásnak legyen saját honlapja" covered web presence costs for 2 years. Its second round closed on 6 Feb 2026 when funds ran out.
  - Széchenyi Card investment loans are available at **3.5%** a year.
  - Sources: [kormany.hu](https://kormany.hu/hirek/indul-a-demjan-sandor-program); [KAVOSZ](https://www.kavosz.hu/kavosz-hirek/elindul-a-demjan-sandor-program-egy-atfogo-kezdemenyezes-a-magyar-kkv-k-fejlodeseert/); [Index 2025-11-01](https://index.hu/gazdasag/2025/11/01/szechenyi-kartya-program-demjan-sandor-tokeprogram-kkv-vallalatok-tamogatas-tamogatott-hitel-kavosz-mkik-cegek/); [berkalkulator](https://berkalkulator.com/hitel/vallalkozoi-hitelek-2026)
- **Hungarian AI Strategy 2025-2030:**
  - Adopted in Sept 2025.
  - Three pillars: "AI for Society", "AI for Technology" (incl. open-source models) and "AI for Business", which specifically targets SME AI integration.
  - Target: 2.5M people with AI competencies by 2030.
  - The AI Coalition (MI Koalíció, 400+ members) is operated by **Neumann János Nonprofit Zrt.**
  - Sources: [kormany.hu strategy PDF](https://cdn.kormany.hu/uploads/document/c/c0/c0d/c0dfdbd37cfa520ae37361a168d244c85e7295af.pdf); [Neum.hu](https://neum.hu/en/magyarorszag-bemutatta-a-2025-2030-as-mesterseges-intelligencia-strategiat/); [VG](https://www.vg.hu/vilaggazdasag-magyar-gazdasag/2025/09/magyar-mi-strategia-kormanyzat-gazdasag-egeszsegugy); [MI Koalíció](https://neum.hu/en/mi/)

### Inferences
- How the provider fits in: grants reimburse the SME's purchases of software and services. An AI-automation firm becomes a vendor on the SME's project, possibly with a grant consultant who takes a success fee.
  - On a HUF 12M project at 90% intensity, the SME pays only about HUF 1.2M plus non-refundable VAT if applicable. This removes most price resistance.
  - Grant-financed purchases usually need formal quotes, deliverables and a sustainability period. The provider needs to be a registered company issuing compliant invoices.
- Szeged is in Dél-Alföld, which is eligible for the regional DIMOP calls (B-type) that exclude Budapest. That is a geographic advantage.
- The provider can join the MI Koalíció as a member for visibility and possibly as an implementer in the "AI for Business" pillar.

### Gaps
- Which DIMOP/GINOP Plusz calls are *open now* (Oct 2026) was not verified. Calls open and close quickly, so palyazat.gov.hu must be checked directly.
- Hungarian EDIHs (European Digital Innovation Hubs) were not researched. Hungary has several, including some with a Szeged / University of Szeged link, but this is unverified.
- Details of the Demján "digitalisation package" beyond the website programme were not found.
- Political risk: after the April 2026 parliamentary elections, government programmes (Demján, the AI strategy's institutions) may be restructured. I did not research their post-election status.

## 5. Price levels and willingness to pay

### Takeaway
The data on what Hungarian SMEs pay is thin. The one usable figure: firms with 5-249 employees spent about HUF 100k/month on average on IT in 2024, and half spend at least HUF 50k/month. Mainstream SaaS per seat runs about HUF 5-6k per user per month.

### Cited Findings
- In 2024 the average monthly IT cost of firms with 5-249 employees rose to almost **HUF 100,000**. Every second firm spends at least **HUF 50,000/month** — [NMHH business survey 2024](https://nmhh.hu/cikk/251191/Az_elektronikus_hirkozlesi_piac_fogyasztoinak_vizsgalata_2024__uzleti_felmeres) (snippet; attribution to NMHH is inferred from the search results and should be checked)
- Reference SaaS prices: Microsoft 365 Standard about **HUF 5,900/user/month**, Teams Premium about HUF 5,300/user/month — [szamlazoprogramom.hu](https://www.szamlazoprogramom.hu/blog/kkv-digitalizacio-2026-ban-gyakorlati-utmutato-a-hatekonysagnoveleshez/) (snippet)

### Inferences
- An AI-automation retainer of HUF 30-150k/month would equal or double a typical SME's whole current IT budget. Selling it needs ROI framed in saved working hours, valued at the HUF 380k-850k monthly employer cost from section 1, or grant co-funding.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable Hungarian survey of willingness to pay for AI services specifically, and no market price list for AI-automation projects.

## 6. Hungarian-language LLMs and language as a moat

### Takeaway
Hungarian research institutions build domestic models:
- NYTK/HUN-REN: PULI GPT-3SX 6.7B, PULI-LlumiX (Llama 3.1-based) and PULI Trio (Qwen 2.5-based).
- Benchmarks: HuLU and HuGME.
- Racka, an academic adaptation published in 2026.

Global frontier models (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) already dominate actual business use in Hungary. Hungarian is therefore a *service and UX* moat (local support, Hungarian documents, NAV/legal context) more than a model moat.

### Cited Findings
- NYTK (HUN-REN Hungarian Research Centre for Linguistics) developed PULI. PULI-GPT-3SX has 6.7B parameters. PULI-LlumiX-Llama-3.1 Chat and PULI Trio Q Chat (Qwen 2.5) are the first Hungarian-focused chat LLMs, with HuCOLA 66.98%, HuSST 70.06% and HuRTE 74.54% — [nytud.hu](https://nytud.hu/hir/irany-a-chatgpt-elkeszult-a-magyar-nyelvi-modell-a-puli); [aiforradalom](https://aiforradalom.poltextlab.com/uttoro-magyar-nyelvmodell-megerkezett-a-puli-llumix/); [real.mtak.hu paper 2025](https://real.mtak.hu/228854/1/261_274_yang.pdf)
- HuLU is a Hungarian Language Understanding benchmark modelled on GLUE/SuperGLUE (LREC 2024). HuGME is a 2025 benchmark for evaluating Hungarian generative models — [ACL LREC 2024](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.733.pdf); [ACL GEM 2025](https://aclanthology.org/2025.gem-1.32.pdf)
- Racka: efficient Hungarian LLM adaptation on academic infrastructure (arXiv 2601.01244, 2026) — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2601.01244)
- Business use in Hungary is dominated by global tools: ChatGPT 69%, Gemini 56%, Claude 37%, Copilot 29% — [Prim](https://hirek.prim.hu/cikk/2025/11/25/mesterseges_intelligencia_a_magyar_cegeknel_hol_tartunk_2025-ben)
- The AI Strategy's "AI for Technology" pillar supports open-source models — [Neum.hu](https://neum.hu/en/magyarorszag-bemutatta-a-2025-2030-as-mesterseges-intelligencia-strategiat/)

### Inferences
- Local models such as PULI could matter for on-premise or data-sovereign deployments where GDPR or NIS2 sensitivity is high. For most SME automation, frontier APIs with Hungarian prompts, Hungarian document templates and local legal and tax knowledge are good enough.
- The defensible moat is domain knowledge of Hungarian processes (NAV online invoicing, eÁFA, KATA/KIVA, Hungarian payroll), Hungarian-speaking support, and access to grant financing. Language alone is not a moat against global vendors.

### Gaps
- I found no head-to-head 2025-26 benchmark comparing PULI with GPT, Claude or Gemini on Hungarian business tasks.
- I found no survey data on whether Hungarian SMEs *require* Hungarian-language AI interfaces, or on their trust and fear levels beyond the VOSZ "lack of expertise" figures.
