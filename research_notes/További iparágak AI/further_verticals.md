# Further Hungarian verticals for the "accounting playbook" (setup fee + monthly fee, Hungarian systems and regulations, network sales), Szeged focus, October 2026

Research date: 2026-10-03. This builds on `reports/AI üzleti lehetőségek Szegeden.md` and `research_notes/AI üzleti lehetőségek Szegeden/` and does not repeat them: accounting, legal research, voice receptionists, tenders monitoring, e-commerce and AI-literacy training are already covered there.

**Method caveat.** The egress proxy blocked almost every Hungarian primary source I tried to fetch (ksh.hu, portfolio.hu, transpack.hu, rsm.hu, hrportal.hu, kormanyhivatalok.hu, teaorszamok.hu, among others). Most figures below therefore come from **search-engine result summaries ("snippet")** of the cited pages, not from reading the pages themselves. Treat every number as "verify before use". I did not use any figure that lacks a source. I also ran out of web-search budget before I could check the driving-school, vet-pricing, Szeged-clinic and haulage-count questions; those are listed under Gaps.

**Scoring key, used in every section.** Criteria C1-C6 are the ones in the assignment:
- C1: many small firms
- C2: Hungarian system or regulatory hook
- C3: labor shortage
- C4: owner-led and can pay HUF 100-500k/month
- C5: weak Hungarian AI competition
- C6: fit with Szeged specifics

Each criterion is scored 1-5, so the maximum is 30. **The scores are my judgement (inference), based on the cited facts.**

## 1. Temporary staffing agencies (munkaerő-kölcsönzők) and guest-worker (vendégmunkás) agencies

### Takeaway
The workflows are heavily document-driven and very Hungary-specific: NFSZ registration, TMUNK/foreign-worker reporting, permits, and the quarterly kölcsönző register. But the segment shrank in 2024-25. On top of that, the new government **stopped issuing new guest-worker residence permits from 6 June 2026**, which turns the guest-worker angle from a growth market into regulatory whiplash. **Verdict: medium-low as a standalone vertical.** It is better repackaged as "foreign-worker and HR compliance back-office" for employers, including BYD suppliers.

### Cited Findings
- **Agency count (old data).** The latest count found is from 2018: 913 temporary staffing companies (counting sites and headquarters together) and 150,746 workers on loan. Agencies must report annually to NFSZ by 31 January, and the register is updated quarterly on munka.hu (snippet; data from 2018) — [Tudástár munka.hu](http://tudastar.munka.hu/engine.aspx?page=showcontent&content=tudastar_stat_nmh); [Kormányhivatal tájékoztató](https://www.kormanyhivatal.hu/download/f/93/b1000/Munkaer%C5%91-k%C3%B6lcs%C3%B6nz%C5%91k%20t%C3%A1j%C3%A9koztat%C3%B3.pdf)
- **Market shrinking.**
  - Workers on loan at the larger agencies fell from more than 235k (2023) to 201.5k (2024), a 7.2% drop.
  - The top 50 agencies went from 52,681 staff (Dec 2023) to 47,568 (Apr 2025).
  - Agency workers' share of total employment fell from about 5% to below 4% (snippet).
  - Sources: [HR Portál 2025-08](https://www.hrportal.hu/hr/igy-allnak-letszamban-a-munkaero-kolcsonzo-cegek-20250801.html); [HR Portál 2025-02](https://www.hrportal.hu/hr/fekete-ev-volt-a-tavalyi-a-nagy-munkaero-kolcsonzoknek-20250213.html); [Népszava](https://nepszava.hu/3286881_kolcsonzott-munkaero-fogyasa-rugalmassag-versenykepesseg)
- **Qualified lenders are few by design.** Since early 2024, qualified-lender (minősített kölcsönbeadó) status requires at least 1,000 staff, of whom at least 500 must be Hungarian citizens. Only qualified lenders and preferred employers could hire guest workers. The quota was 65k in 2024 and 35k in 2025 (snippet) — [jobtain.hu](https://jobtain.hu/mit-jelent-ma-magyarorszagon-minositett-munkaero-kolcsonzonek-lenni/); [HR Portál](https://www.hrportal.hu/hr/ok-hozhatnak-be-kulfoldi-vendegmunkasokat-minositett-foglalkoztatokent-20221118.html)
- **The 2026 policy shock.**
  - Since **6 June 2026**, no new guest-worker residence permit can be requested. Existing permits can be extended, and applications filed before 6 June are decided under the old rules.
  - Employment-purpose residence permits (foglalkoztatási célú tartózkodási engedély) remain, but are barred for citizens of Georgia, Armenia and the Philippines.
  - Sources: [Veszprémi Kamara](https://veszpremikamara.hu/hirek/tajekoztato-a-vendegmunkas-szabalyozas-modositasarol); [Telex 2026-06-05](https://telex.hu/gazdasag/2026/06/05/vendegmunkas-torveny-orszaglista-szigoritas-eltorles-fulop-szigetek); [Deloitte](https://www.deloitte.com/hu/hu/services/tax/perspectives/fontos-valtozasok-a-vendegmunkasok-foglalkoztatasara-vonatkozo-szabalyokban.html); [Pénzcentrum](https://www.penzcentrum.hu/karrier/20260605/hivatalos-jon-a-vendegmunkas-szigor-megjelent-a-rendelet-ok-mar-nem-dolgozhatnak-magyarorszagon-1199971)
- **Contested effect.** Világgazdaság (2026-07) claims Filipino arrivals continue through other permit routes, so the effect of the "stop" is disputed — [VG 2026-07](https://www.vg.hu/vilaggazdasag-magyar-gazdasag/2026/07/vendegmunkasstop-fulop-szigetek-magyar-peter)
- **Foreign-worker reporting rules** changed and tightened from 2025 — [HR Portál 2025-03](https://www.hrportal.hu/hr/kulfoldi-munkavallalok-bejelentese-valtozo-szabalyok-fontos-tudnivalok-20250319.html); [propeller.hu](https://propeller.hu/kkv-hirek/4198434-valtozik-kulfoldi-munkavallalok-bejelentese-2025-tol-szigorubbak-szabalyok)
- **Paper-based processes.** Labor demand and permit requests go through ePapír to the county employment department, under an expedited placement procedure — [NFSZ](https://nfsz.munka.hu/cikk/4163/Munkaeroigeny_es_kerelem_a_konnyitett_kozvetitesi_eljarashoz)
- **No dedicated Hungarian AI tool found.** A search for software or automation for guest-worker administration found only law firms and recruitment intermediaries (for example Overseas Labour, which uses a direct-hire model), not software vendors — [overseaslabour.hu](https://overseaslabour.hu/kulfoldi-munkaero-alkalmazas-2026-magyarorszag/); [drnagyildiko.hu](https://drnagyildiko.hu/blog/vendegmunkas-statusz-tartozkodasi-engedely-2026/)
- **Horticulture depends on this labor.** Producers warn that greenhouse investments stop without guest workers, and say the cut to the 120-day simplified-employment limit makes seasonal hiring harder — [Agroinform](https://www.agroinform.hu/kerteszet_szoleszet/legyen-vendegmunkas-kulonben-leallnak-a-kerteszeti-beruhazasok-93991-001); [Magyar Mezőgazdaság 2025-11](https://magyarmezogazdasag.hu/2025/11/26/tovabb-novekszik-a-szentesi-szovetkezet/)

### Inferences
- **Most automatable workflows:**
  - permit and renewal file assembly and deadline tracking (permit expiries, medical checks, housing documents)
  - multilingual onboarding and worker Q&A (HU/EN/SR/Asian languages)
  - payroll inputs and timesheets for leased workers, plus the NFSZ annual report
- **Willingness to pay:** small agencies are probably in the HUF 100-300k/month range (no source).
- **Main risk is policy volatility.** Rules changed in 2024, 2025 and June 2026. A product bound to the guest-worker permit route can lose its market overnight. A rules engine that is cheap to update is the only defensible design.
- **Szeged angle:** BYD's ramp-up, Serbian cross-border commuters and Szentes horticulture all need foreign or seasonal labor administration. That points to selling to **employers**, not only to agencies.
- **Scores:** C1 2 · C2 5 · C3 3 · C4 3 · C5 4 · C6 4 = **21/30**

### Gaps
- No 2024-2026 count of registered staffing agencies, and none for Csongrád-Csanád. The munka.hu register could not be accessed.
- I could not find out how many guest workers are in Csongrád-Csanád, or how many permits BYD or its suppliers use.

## 2. Freight forwarders, hauliers and customs brokers (Röszke / METRANS / BYD)

### Takeaway
This is the strongest Szeged-specific vertical. Röszke is the EU-Serbia (non-EU) border, with recurring 8-10 hour queues, blockades and missing-declaration checks. METRANS is building a 300k TEU terminal next to BYD, due in 2027. The customs IT stack is changing (eVÁM, and automated customs decisions since 31 July 2026). The incumbent software (SpediTrans, GLI) is ERP or route planning, not AI document handling. Margins are thin, though: about half of hauliers ran at a loss in early 2025. **Verdict: top-tier.** Lead with customs brokers and forwarders, which are document-heavy and have higher margins than pure hauliers.

### Cited Findings
- **Röszke congestion.** There were 8-10 hour waits at Röszke in August 2026, and truck blockades closed Röszke, Tompa and Hercegszántó to freight in January 2026 — [Origo 2026-08](https://www.origo.hu/belpol/2026/08/roszke-hataratkeles-varakozas-kaosz); [Szegedma 2026-01](https://szegedma.hu/tersegi-hirek/2026/01/kamionblokad-teherforgalom-roszke-hatar-lezaras); [Index 2026-01](https://index.hu/belfold/2026/01/23/magyarorszag-szerbia-roszke-tompa-hercegszanto-hatar-atkelo-teherforgalom-korlatozas-lezaras/); [Infostart](https://infostart.hu/kulfold/2026/01/27/kamionblokaddal-tiltakoznak-a-delszlav-fuvarosok-a-magyar-szerb-hatarokon)
- **Practitioner advice on Röszke/Horgos.**
  - EU-third country customs procedure is compulsory, with multi-hour peaks.
  - On some days up to 2,000 trucks wait at Tompa and Röszke.
  - Trucks often arrive without an entry summary declaration lodged, which triggers an immediate customs check.
  - T1 transit with seals means stricter checks and requires a customs agent prepared in advance.
  - Source (snippet, vendor blog): [kamionshopkft.hu](https://www.kamionshopkft.hu/hataratlepes-es-vamkezeles-kamionnal)
- **Customs IT and law changes.**
  - eVÁM replaced CDPS for import declarations from 1 Jan 2024. It can process up to 500k declarations in 24 hours.
  - From **31 July 2026**, automated decision-making (no customs officer involvement) is allowed for some electronically filed declarations.
  - Sources: [Andersen](https://hu.andersen.com/hu/hirek/parkolopalyan-a-cdps-szervusz-evam/); [szak-ma.hu](https://szak-ma.hu/ado/evam-az-uj-arunyilatkozatfeldolgozo-rendszer-103359); [iriszoffice.hu](https://iriszoffice.hu/egypercesek/gyorsabba-valhat-a-vameljaras-ujabb-lepes-az-adminisztracio-csokkentese-fele/); [NavigátorVilág 2026 customs IT](https://navigatorvilag.hu/hir/mvsz-2/); [NavigátorVilág](https://navigatorvilag.hu/cikk/nv/szallkonf-2025-9/)
- **NAV enforcement focus.** NAV's 2026 audit plan uses AI-supported risk selection. In customs it focuses on non-EU trade, sanctions compliance and product safety — [NAV](https://nav.gov.hu/sajtoszoba/hirek/Vagujhelyi_Ferenc_a_kockazatelemzes_es_mesterseges_intelligencia_tamogatta_kivalasztasra_epulnek_a_2026-os_ellenorzesek); [KPMG taxalert 2026-02](https://kpmg.com/hu/hu/home/tanulmanyok/2026/02/taxalert-2026-02-26.html)
- **METRANS terminal.**
  - Kiskundorozsma site, about HUF 6bn, with HUF 860M state aid, due by 2027.
  - Capacity of 6 train pairs a day and 300k TEU a year.
  - Waberer's terminal will handle BYD finished-car rail shipments.
  - BYD expects about 1,000 cars a day, about 700 of them leaving by rail.
  - Sources: [Magyar Építők 2025-12](https://magyarepitok.hu/mi-epul/2025/12/europa-egyik-legmodernebb-logisztikai-terminalja-epul-szegeden-a-byd-gyar-mellett); [magyarvasut.hu](https://magyarvasut.hu/uj-intermodalis-kontenerterminal-epul-szegeden-elindult-a-metrans-csoport-300-000-kontener-ev-kapacitasu-beruhazasa/); [szeged365 2026-07](https://szeged365.hu/2026/07/13/a-byd-szegedi-gyaraban-naponta-varhatoan-1000-auto-keszul-majd-amelyek-kozul-mintegy-700-at-vasuton-szallitanak-el/); [Index 2026-07](https://index.hu/belfold/2026/07/13/byd-autogyar-szeged-kiskundorozsma-maganut/)
- **Szeged-Röszke rail project.** Physical completion was scheduled for 31 Aug 2026 — [MÁV](https://www.mavcsoport.hu/mav-csoport/szeged-roszke-projekt)
- **Haulier economics.**
  - Hauliers have been leaving the market since 2022, roughly 9.1-10.5% of them, with about a 2% drop in Q2 2025 alone (DigiLog, via snippet).
  - About half of hauliers were loss-making in Q1 2025.
  - Sources: [Transpack 2025-09](https://transpack.hu/2025/09/26/logisztika-csokken-fuvarozasi-vallalkozasok-szama/); [VG 2025-02](https://www.vg.hu/vilaggazdasag-magyar-gazdasag/2025/02/fuvarozo-kamion-digilog-kfx); [beszerzes.hu](https://beszerzes.hu/2025/05/14/2025-sem-a-kozuti-fuvarozok-eve-lesz/)
- **Incumbent software (not AI):**
  - SpediTrans is forwarding and haulage software used by about 300 companies over 20 years, with orders and multilingual confirmations — [speditrans.hu](https://www.speditrans.hu/)
  - GLI Solutions does route planning — [Műszaki Magazin](https://www.muszaki-magazin.hu/2022/01/26/fuvareszervezes-optimalizalas-gli/)
  - No Hungarian AI startup for freight email, quote or customs document automation was found.

### Inferences
- **Automatable workflows:**
  1. Customs brokers: turning commercial invoices, packing lists and CMRs into draft eVÁM/NCTS/ENS declarations, with HS-code suggestions and a check for missing entry summary declarations before trucks reach Röszke.
  2. Forwarders: an email/WhatsApp quote-request inbox (extraction, a price-quote draft, an order created in SpediTrans or another TMS).
  3. Haulier back office: driver-document collection (CMR photos, then invoicing), Mobility Package and posting paperwork, and e-útdíj toll invoice reconciliation. The Mobility Package and e-útdíj hooks are inferred and were not source-checked in this session.
- **Willingness to pay:** customs brokers and forwarders are probably HUF 150-400k/month, hauliers HUF 50-150k because of thin margins. No source; this is an estimate.
- **Szeged edge:** physical presence at Röszke and at the METRANS/BYD logistics hub, plus Serbian-language capability, is something a Budapest agency cannot easily copy.
- **Scores:** C1 4 · C2 5 · C3 4 · C4 3 · C5 4 · C6 5 = **25/30**

### Gaps
- There is no current count of licensed hauliers, forwarders or customs representatives nationally or in Csongrád-Csanád; the TEÁOR 4941/5229 counts were blocked. My search budget ran out before I could check MKFE/NiT figures.
- No prices for Hungarian customs-broker software were found.

## 3. Condominium managers (társasházkezelők / közös képviselők)

### Takeaway
The market is large: about 1.5M flats are in condominiums, and condominium managers must now be registered in the land registry. But the software layer is mature and is already bundling AI cheaply. eHÁZ (about 13k condominiums) gives the THT legal AI assistant free to all its managers and is testing a resident-facing AI customer service. Thaz-SofT includes an AI email assistant and an AI voice assistant. **Verdict: medium.** It works only as a done-for-you layer over eHÁZ or Thaz-SofT (handling the inbox and phone, minutes of general meetings, debt collection letters) for mid-size Szeged managers, or as a partnership with the software vendors.

### Cited Findings
- **Size.** Hungary has about 4.4M dwellings, of which nearly 1.5M are in condominiums run by elected managers or management firms (snippet) — [Economx](https://www.economx.hu/magyar-gazdasag/kozos-kepviselo-ingatlanugyi-hatosag-nyilvantartas.788334.html); [Infostart 2024-04](https://infostart.hu/belfold/2024/04/16/nagy-valtozas-jon-a-tarsashazakban-veszelyben-a-kozos-kepviselok-ha-nem-lepnek)
- **New registration duty.** Managers of condominiums with more than 6 flats had to register with the land registry from 1 Oct 2024, with a deadline of 1 May 2025 — [WHM Cloud](https://whmcloud.hu/hirek/kozos-kepviselo-foldhivatali-regisztracio/); [jogaszvilag.hu](https://jogaszvilag.hu/szakma/nyilvantartasba-kell-venni-a-tarsashazi-kozos-kepviseloket/); [Szegedi Ügyvédi Kamara 2025-02](https://www.szegediugyvedikamara.hu/hirek/2025-02-25_t%C3%A1j%C3%A9koztat%C3%A1s-t%C3%A1rsash%C3%A1zakkal-kapcsolatos-t%C3%A9nyek-bejegyz%C3%A9se-vonatkoz%C3%A1s%C3%A1ban/)
- **Szeged-specific change.** From 1 Jan 2025 condominium waste billing changed in Szeged, which adds manager admin — [Szegedi Hulladékgazdálkodási](https://www.szegedihulladek.hu/ugyfelszolgalat/tarsashazi-szamlazas)
- **Incumbent AI.**
  - The THT legal AI assistant is free for every manager on eHÁZ. It is trained on the condominium act, the Civil Code (Ptk.), accounting rules, land-registry law, two decades of Curia (supreme court) decisions and 25 years of THT archive.
  - eHÁZ is testing an AI customer service on the owner portal that answers questions about balances and payments.
  - From 2026 the AI is open to all THT subscribers.
  - Sources: [THT](https://www.tht.hu/hirek/mesterseges-intelligencia-a-tarsashazkezelesben-elindult-az-ehaz-jogi-ai-asszisztens-es-jon-a-lakoi-ugyfelszolgalat-is/2909); [THT 2026 cooperation](https://www.tht.hu/hirek/mitoe-tht-egyuttmukodes-2026-ban-minden-tht-elofizeto-szamara-megnyilik-a-tht-tarsashazi-es-ingatlanpiaci-szakmai-mesterseges-intelligencia-hasznalata/2846)
- **Software prices (snippets):**
  - Thaz-SofT: from HUF 99,000 perpetual licence, HUF 0/month, including an AI email assistant and AI voice assistant — [tarsashazaink.hu](https://tarsashazaink.hu/)
  - OnlineHáz: HUF 49 + VAT per unit per month — [onlinehaz.hu](https://onlinehaz.hu/)
  - Társasház App: HUF 39 net per unit per month — [tarsashaz.app](https://tarsashaz.app/)
  - Other registry software: HUF 5,400-43,000 + VAT a month — [nyilvantarto-program.hu](https://nyilvantarto-program.hu/termek/tarsashazkezelo-szoftver/)
- **Szeged market.** Szeged has many manager firms, ranging from single managers to firms founded in 1996, and from panel blocks to new residential parks. No count was found — [joszaki Szeged](https://joszaki.hu/szakemberek/tarsashazkezeles/szeged); [szakkatalogus](https://www.szakkatalogus.hu/Szeged-2736/T%C3%A1rsash%C3%A1zkezel%C3%A9s-2124); [tarsashazkezelo-szeged.hu](https://www.tarsashazkezelo-szeged.hu/)
- **Fee benchmarks:** [joszaki árak](https://joszaki.hu/arak/kozos-kepviselet)

### Inferences
- Software is priced at HUF 39-49 per unit per month. A manager with 2,000 units pays about HUF 80-100k/month for software, so a HUF 150-300k/month AI service needs to replace a clerk's time, not a tool.
- **Workflows:**
  1. The resident inbox and phone (fault reports, then contractor tickets).
  2. General-meeting packs: invitation, minutes drafted from recordings, resolutions log.
  3. Arrears letters escalating to a payment order (fizetési meghagyás, MOKK) and building-renovation grant paperwork.
- **Scores:** C1 5 · C2 4 · C3 3 · C4 3 · C5 2 · C6 3 = **20/30**

## 4. Construction contractors (e-építési napló, subcontractor documents, tenders)

### Takeaway
The sector is very large: 149.5k registered economic organizations, and 56.6k operating companies in August 2026, a number that is shrinking. It is under heavy regulatory pressure: e-napló changes in 2026, the requirement to show the main contractor's registration number in subcontracts, and labor inspections finding 82.2% of workers employed irregularly. It also has a severe labor shortage. Digital maturity and margins are low. **Verdict: good for mid-size general contractors (20-200 staff)**, including those around BYD/METRANS. It is not a good fit for micro builders.

### Cited Findings
- **Firm counts.**
  - 149.5k registered economic organizations in construction at end-2025, flat year on year.
  - Operating companies fell from 61.5k (end-2022) to 57.5k (end-2025) and 56.6k (Aug 2026).
  - About 140k construction enterprises in total, including sole proprietors.
  - All contractors must register in the MKIK contractor register.
  - Sources (snippet): [hirnavigator/KSH graph](https://hirnavigator.hu/hir/10782636-a-regisztralt-gazdasagi-szervezetek-szama-az-epitoiparban-grafikon); [Portfolio 2026-09](https://www.portfolio.hu/ingatlan/20260909/a-kapacitas-keszen-all-mar-csak-a-fellendulesre-var-az-epitoipar-861420); [MKIK GYIK](https://mkik.hu/epitoipari-kivitelezo-vallalkozasok-gyik); [BKIK](https://bkik.hu/epitoipari-regisztracio)
- **Compliance pressure.** In 2025 labor inspectors checked 1,885 construction firms and found irregularities at 67.9% of them, with 82.2% of workers in irregular employment — [Infostart 2026-09-14](https://infostart.hu/gazdasag/2026/09/14/koji-laszlo-az-arenaban-az-epitoiparban-822-szazalekos-a-problematikus-foglalkoztatottsag)
- **e-napló changes in 2026:**
  - new design-contract log types from 1 April
  - a role was renamed on 25 Feb 2026
  - subcontracts must contain the main contractor's registration number, for subcontractor-chain transparency
  - a mobile app refresh from end-September 2026
  - Sources: [e-epites.hu](https://www.e-epites.hu/hirek/e-epitesi-naplo/tajekoztato-az-e-epitesi-naplo-alkalmazast-erinto-valtozasrol); [e-epites.hu 2](https://www.e-epites.hu/hirek/e-epitesi-naplo/tajekoztato-az-e-epitesi-naplot-erinto-valtozasrol); [MEK 2026-01](https://mek.hu/link-ekm_tajekoztato_az_epitesugyi_targyu_kormanyrendeletek_modositasarol_2026_januar_8); [BPMK e-napló 2026 slides](https://bpmkkepzesek.hu/images/2026/BPMK_E-NAPLO_2026.04_-ki.2.pdf); [Aggteleki Consulting](https://aggtelekiconsulting.hu/epitesi-jogszabalyok-2026/)
- **Sector mood.** Business sentiment is weak and companies expect another hard year — [ÉVOSZ konjunktúra](https://evosz.hu/hirek/1148-konjunkturafelmeres2025ii); [Épületgépész 2025-05](https://epuletgepesz.hu/2025/05/21/epitoipar-gki-kutatas-vallalkozas-2025-i-negyedev/); [Piac&Profit](https://piacesprofit.hu/cikkek/gazdasag/ujabb-nehez-evre-keszulnek-az-epitoipari-vallalatok-.html)
- **Public procurement thresholds 2026 (unchanged).** For classic contracting authorities: goods HUF 20M, works HUF 60M, services HUF 20M. Construction firms bid through EKR (the electronic procurement system) — [Közbeszerzési Hatóság](https://kozbeszerzes.hu/kozbeszerzesek-az/magyar-jogi-hatter/elnoki-tajekoztatok/a-kozbeszerzesi-hatosag-elnokenek-tajekoztatoja-a-2026-januar-1-jetol-iranyado-kozbeszerzesi-ertekhatarokrol/)
- **Competition.** No Hungarian AI product aimed at e-napló entries or subcontractor-document checks was found. Tender-monitoring AI tools exist (see the previous report).

### Inferences
- **Workflows:**
  1. Daily e-napló entries drafted from foreman voice notes and photos, which the responsible site manager then approves.
  2. Subcontractor compliance pack: MKIK registration check, NAV köztartozásmentes adatbázis (tax-debt-free taxpayer database) check, employee lists, A1/posting documents, contract chain with the main contractor's registration number.
  3. Tender/EKR bid document assembly and price-list (költségvetés) comparison.
- **Willingness to pay:** owner-led mid-size GCs are probably HUF 150-400k/month. Micro firms will not pay.
- **Szeged angle:** BYD plant expansion, the METRANS terminal, the Szeged-Röszke rail project and road works bring many subcontractor chains into the county.
- **Scores:** C1 5 · C2 4 · C3 5 · C4 3 · C5 4 · C6 4 = **25/30**. Note that C4 is weak for small firms.

### Gaps
- No Csongrád-Csanád count of construction firms.

## 12. Score summary and ranked top 5

### Takeaway
Ranked by fit with the playbook (HU-specific hook, Szeged advantage, ability to pay, weak AI competition):
1. Customs and forwarding around Röszke/METRANS
2. Agri-food cooperatives, integrators and advisers (Szentes cluster), with food-processor quality documentation as the upsell

### Inferences
**Ranked top 5 and why:**

1. **Customs brokers and forwarders (Röszke/Tompa, METRANS, BYD logistics).**
   - Why: a unique local pain (an EU external border with hours-long queues), a changing state IT stack (eVÁM, automated decisions from 31 Jul 2026), and documents arriving as messy multilingual PDFs, emails and photos.
   - Competition: no AI competitor found; incumbents (SpediTrans, GLI) are ERPs.
   - Pilot: "pre-arrival document check plus draft declaration" for 2-3 Szeged/Röszke customs agencies.
   - Risk: thin haulier margins, so price toward brokers and forwarders.
2. **Agri-food cluster: Szentes greenhouse cooperatives, TÉSZ, integrators, KAP advisers; upsell to Pick/Sole-Mizo-type processors.**
   - Why: the highest Szeged fit, compulsory Hungarian paperwork (KAP/MePAR, simplified employment, plant-protection logs) plus GlobalG.A.P./IFS, and an acute labor shortage.
   - Selling: sell to organizations that aggregate many farmers; use the salesperson's network for warm intros.
   - Risk: seasonality and an agri-literacy requirement.
3. **Mid-size construction contractors.**
   - Why: a large base, new 2026 e-napló and subcontract-chain rules, inspections finding widespread irregularities, and BYD/METRANS/rail projects generating local subcontractor chains.
   - Products: e-napló drafting from voice and photos, a subcontractor compliance pack, EKR bid assembly.
   - Risk: low digital maturity, so target firms with 20+ staff.
4. **Private dental clinics: integration layer for follow-up, tourism leads and documentation.**
   - Why: about 2,600 businesses that pay out of pocket, with compulsory EESZT reporting.
   - Positioning: the crowded voice-receptionist niche and the existing AI scribe (MedAssist) mean a done-for-you integration on top of Flexi-Dent, Cloudent or DentRP, aimed at revenue recovery (recall, unaccepted treatment plans, foreign-patient pipeline incl. Serbian).
5. **Payroll bureaus plus employers' foreign-worker and HR compliance.**
   - Why: it shares the accounting sales motion and NAV hooks.
   - Guest-worker angle: the June 2026 guest-worker stop and the tightening of employment-purpose permits create recurring compliance work for employers (BYD suppliers, horticulture) in a changing rule set. The agency-only version is too exposed to policy swings.

**Honourable mentions:**
- NIS2 as a cross-sell to the same mid-size manufacturers and food processors.
- Condominium managers only as a partner layer on eHÁZ or Thaz-SofT.
